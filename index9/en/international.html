Connections across borders.

I plan and run campaigns that reach specialists and decision-makers in other markets – from Europe to the USA and the Middle East.

Book an intro callPrice

The situation

Entering new markets or looking for rare specialists means you know nobody there. Cold mass emails don’t work, and time zones, languages and cultures make outreach hard.

What you get

  • Clear target groups: roles, regions, organisations
  • Researched contact lists of suitable experts and decision-makers
  • Personal, multilingual outreach instead of mass mailing
  • Intro meetings booked into your calendar
  • Ongoing reporting: who was contacted, who replied
  • Handover of all contacts and meeting notes
CaseAn example: for an international healthcare project to establish Lu-177 PSMA therapy centres in the USA and the Middle East, I identified suitable experts, approached them and organised intro meetings. The client then placed a follow-up order.

How it works

  1. 1week 1

    Define targets

    Who do you want to reach, where, and to what end?

  2. 2week 1–2

    Research

    Identify and qualify organisations and contacts.

  3. 3week 2–4

  4. 4ongoing

  5. 5project end

Frequently asked questions

Which regions do you cover?

Europe, the USA and the Middle East. Outreach in German, Italian, English or French.

Isn’t this just mass email?

No. Every message is researched and personal. It’s about a few suitable contacts, not many.
