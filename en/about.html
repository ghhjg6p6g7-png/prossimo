Dario Della Porta.

Founder & CEO of Prossimo Business Solutions. Recruiter, organiser and technologist in one person.

I have spent more than eight years in recruiting, HR and operations – at a Fortune 500 technology company, at Switzerland’s largest telecom provider, in staffing, in industry and at one of Europe’s leading PV manufacturers.

On top of that come a bachelor’s degree from the University of Applied Sciences Hamburg and a business diploma focused on project, process and people management. Prossimo grew out of this mix: I find the right people and build the processes that take work off teams.

Raised with Italian and German, fluent in English and French. Today I live and work in Toulon and serve clients across Europe.

I don’t believe in one-size-fits-all consulting. Every business has its own rhythm – my job is to find yours and accelerate it.Dario Della Porta · Founder & CEO, Prossimo Business Solutions
