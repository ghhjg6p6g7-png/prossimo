From detours to workflows.

I analyse how your team works, standardise processes across sites and coordinate projects until they are delivered.

Book an intro callPrice

The situation

Every site does it differently, information gets lost, projects fizzle out. Not because people work badly, but because the process is missing.

What you get

  • A clear view of today’s workflows and bottlenecks
  • Standardised processes across teams and sites
  • Documentation your team actually uses
  • Coordination of projects across countries and languages
  • Metrics that show progress
  • Supported rollout instead of a report in a drawer

How it works

  1. 1week 1–2

    Analysis

    Interviews, observe workflows, make bottlenecks visible.

  2. 2week 2–3

    Target process

    Design a simpler workflow and agree it with the team.

  3. 3week 3–6

    Rollout

    Introduce templates, roles and tools, and train people.

  4. 4ongoing

    Coordination

    Keep people, dates and decisions together.

  5. 5after 4–8 weeks

    Review

    Check what works and refine.

Deliverables

  • Process map
  • Target processes
  • Templates and checklists
  • Roles and responsibilities
  • Project plan
  • Review report
