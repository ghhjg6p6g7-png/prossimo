Frequently asked questions
Which technologies do you use?
Python, SQL, JavaScript and AI models (LLMs) – always suited to the task and your existing systems.
Is AI allowed in recruiting?
Yes, with rules. The EU AI Act classifies AI in employment decisions as high-risk. That’s why AI only prepares in my work – people decide.
What happens to our data?
Confidential data goes to AI services only with your consent. Where possible, solutions run on your side.
